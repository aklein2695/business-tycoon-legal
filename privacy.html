Privacy Policy — Business Tycoon

Last updated: 14 June 2026

Business Tycoon ("the app") is a single-player simulation game. We take your privacy seriously: the app does not collect, store, or share any personal data.

Data

  • No personal information is collected. There is no account, login, or sign-up.
  • Your game progress is saved locally on your device only. It never leaves your device and is removed if you delete the app.
  • The app contains no advertising, no analytics, and no third-party tracking.

Purchases

Optional in-app purchases (gem packs) are processed entirely by Apple. We never receive or store your payment information. See Apple's privacy policy for details.

Children

The app is rated 4+ and is suitable for all ages. It does not knowingly collect any data from anyone, including children.
